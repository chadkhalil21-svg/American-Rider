# South Florida market pricing: lawful public-price watch and comparison limits
**12 October 2026 · research-only, scheduled, no Fare changes.**

## First check the evidence, then design

- **Miami-Dade County official taxicab passenger-information page**: https://www.miamidade.gov/global/economy/consumer-protection/taxicabs-passenger-information.page . Its current text describes a maximum meter fare of $2.95 first 1/6 mile, $0.85 each additional 1/6 to one mile, and **$0.40** each 1/6 beyond.
- **Miami-Dade County later legislative analysis**: https://www.miamidade.gov/govaction/matter.asp?file=true&fileAnalysis=false&matter=251162&yearFolder=Y2025 . Its *current maximum* section describes $2.95 and $0.85 for initial units, then **$0.55** each 1/6 beyond. The county pages thus conflict: automatically adopting either as undisputed current law or a consumer-price benchmark would be unsound. Proposed vs enacted legislative history also needs verifying.
- **NBER working paper w34441**: https://www.nber.org/papers/w34441 compares identical routes/times and finds sizeable rideshare price dispersion. A public taxi maximum tariff is **not** a quoted ordinary TNC/ridehail total; a single price screenshot, promotional quote, rate card or taxi meter cannot prove a 10%-below-market claim across South Florida.
- **Google Cloud Firestore pricing**: https://cloud.google.com/firestore/pricing bills reads and writes. A stored weekly checkpoint in the existing daily leased maintenance schedule avoids a full prices-table scan, per-Travel polling and unnecessary persistent writes.
- **AWS Builders' Library idempotency**: https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/ recommends safe repeat operations. Repeating the weekly read will not multiply fare changes or trigger new purchases.
- **NASA SWE-055 validation**: https://swehb.nasa.gov/spaces/SWEHBVD/pages/102695440/SWE-055%2B-%2BRequirements%2BValidation requires evidence that the product behaves in intended conditions. Simulations here DO NOT validate a live independent South Florida TNC quote provider.
- **HBS Frances Frei, service excellence**: https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?research=7002 calls for funding the promised service. **Stanford d.school** https://dschool.stanford.edu/tools/design-thinking-bootleg emphasizes testing early. Ford/Rockefeller/Jobs/Apple and other Council institutions are a research/rubric, not actual advisory consultations.

## What this release does

`backend/south-florida-public-rates.js` **automatically watches two fixed official Miami-Dade government public tariff pages** using no login, subscription, AI or paid provider. At most two bounded HTTPS requests (8 sec timeout, 300 kB response limit, redirect disallowed) are issued on one persisted weekly cycle, through the already running `marketReference` lease and 24-hour maintenance cadence. The watch writes a single `market_public_rate_watch/fl-miami-dade` status when due, even after restarts. The result explicitly records `taxicab_maximum_tariff_not_rideshare_quote`, source hashes, inconsistent values (`source_conflict`), and `rateAuthority:false`, `travelerTotalComparable:false`, `pricePromotionAllowed:false`. On HTML format change, 403, network timeout, oversize, or Firestore error it fails closed as `unverified`. Duplicate/no-due requests do not hit either publisher.

**This is a source-watch and integrity step, not a competitive TNC price collector.** It does not update `market_reference_snapshots`, `market_reference_candidates`, production price coefficients, market admission, Traveler totals or Stripe. No scraping of named commercial marketplaces is introduced. The existing `market-evidence-collectors.js` continues to have Chicago as its only *automated TNC-trip* public dataset. Public county taxi tariffs are structural context only, never 90%-of-market fare authority.

Broward and Palm Beach remain `not_commissioned` for an official public tariff watch until genuine suitable issuer pages/data licenses are identified; **do not inherit Miami values**. South Florida `fl-miami-dade`, `fl-broward`, `fl-palm-beach` already have *plans* for controlled consent-based field panels and optional independent pricing evidence, but no installed approved data feed currently supplies full contemporaneous TNC totals.

## Optional real authorized quote feed: ready for connection, not yet connected

`backend/south-florida-authorized-panel.js` integrates with the existing `configuredCollectors()`/daily `runMarketReferenceSweep()` **only if a lawful full-price data source is configured and its pricing-research rights documented**. It is nationwide-template architecture with three distinct market IDs, never a Miami rate applied to Broward or Palm Beach. These four values are server-side administrative configuration, not traveler-facing data:

- `AR_PANEL_APPROVED_FOR_MARKET_RESEARCH=true` — explicit authorization after reviewing supplier's legal right to provide and license quote observations
- `AR_PANEL_APPROVAL_REFERENCE` — review identifier to match the supplier's response (not a claim of regulator approval)
- `AR_PANEL_HTTPS_URL` — approved HTTPS data-feed endpoint, never a private network address, user-provided URL or unauthenticated commercial marketplace
- `AR_PANEL_READ_TOKEN` — supplier-issued credential for a read-only feed, stored as secret; never in GitHub, logs, tickets or chat

The adapter makes no network request and registers no collector until all four are present. Every source quote must also have a **stable anonymous source observation ID** and a distinct provider key. The existing Firestore ingester uses these as the durable identity, reads an existing document only for retries and **refuses changes to the amount or conditions under the same ID**; the previously published historical public-data IDs remain unchanged. This prevents same-time/same-price supplier observations from collapsing into a single record and prevents quota-consuming duplicate writes. Once connected, at most **50 recent, ordinary, USD all-in, route/time-matched quotes per county per daily collection** can pass. It rejects personal identifiers, promotional/partial prices, absent right-to-use acknowledgement, stale/unverified route conditions, nonapproved markets and suspicious network redirects. The observation type remains a single field-panel source family: the existing independent-source, per-cell minimum, validation and manual promotion gates still apply. A single supplier feeding three counties does **not** become three independent suppliers. No market fare changes automatically.

**This release does NOT connect a legally authorized outside live price-feed supplier or verify ordinary market discounts.** It implements the safe adapter so a future authorized feed requires source commissioning/configuration, not a rewrite of the platform. `backend/south-florida-authorized-panel.test.js` tests all three county paths twice, plus rejection and provider outages.

## Required tests and next actions

`backend/south-florida-public-rates.test.js` uses real production module and fictitious HTTP/Firestore doubles. It runs two independent cycles for rate conflicts, valid identical rates still forbidden from TNC promotion, weekly skip and persistence after restart, offline/oversized source and no per-Travel queries. Added to the full commissioning manifest; existing six release checks, mobile builds, Firestore emulator and twice-run stress campaign remain mandatory. No genuine government page was fetched **by the GitHub synthetic test**.

To actually operate a 90%-of-market condition-matched TNC comparison across the three counties, commission permissible, contemporaneous, total-price observations from at least two independent source families (e.g. permitted public study/panel + genuinely independent audit/licensed feed) at typical route/weekday/daypart cells. Verify sampling consent/terms, service class, route geometry, ordinary promotions/tolls, total included fees and cost floor. Do not automate use of restricted marketplace apps without permission, or treat multiple promotional screenshots as a sufficient distribution. Run holdout/economics/router validation and separately **promote verified cells** using already implemented authorization, then check actual server-side quotes.

**Bottom line:** real public taxi source-change automation can run at low cost, but a genuine self-sufficient live TNC comparison source for all three counties is **not connected or proved**. Do not claim it is or quietly adjust pricing.
