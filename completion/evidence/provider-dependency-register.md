**Alternatives not adopted:** managed PostGIS/Redis may ease geo/presence scale; Onfleet/Shippo/EasyPost are merely courier candidates, not integrated capabilities; a managed case/on-call tool might help human response but does not supply staff. Do not bill unintegrated alternatives as the current architecture.

**Admission and payments update:** A versioned per-county `market_admission` record now separates low-cost waitlist, evidence-authorized prelaunch Operator intake and fully activated commercial Travel. Each proof is bound to the exact source manifest; a changed source requires re-review. Each new offer/reoffer and on-duty renewal reads current admission in its Firestore transaction; the scheduler and reoffer sweeps share one status lookup per market per tick, not one lookup per Travel. A county pause starts bounded on-duty fleet cleanup: up to 200 on-duty reads per page, five pages per market worker tick, with unfinished work remaining an explicit activation blocker. At most four pending counties are scanned per tick. Actual index build, production read/write costs and contention must be measured. A stored Operations reference cannot prove that a policy is bound, a permit was issued or a safety desk is staffed. Stripe dispute and Connect-restriction events now retry if a required state write fails; alert-requiring events also require an accepted, durable, idempotent case. The capture-versus-manual-authorization tradeoff and card validity windows are documented in the [capture decision](stripe-capture-decision.md); no provider or merchant policy change has been executed.

**Founder exception console and disputes:** The previous Operations home fetched all historical `rides` and complete fleet, reservation, ticket and qualification collections on every opening. Its new ten filtered Firestore queries return at most 600 records combined; saturated samples are labelled rather than passed off as exact daily totals. A signed dispute additionally creates one minimal internal evidence packet and a named-Ops-only, 50-item paged review view. This adds per-exception writes/reads but avoids a potentially million-document page-open bill. Source fakes and a PII-free phone render passed; live indexes, case triage at >50 outstanding exceptions, durable evidence retention/legal holds and actual Stripe dispute representment are **not verified**.

## Common monthly demand scenario — inherited, not an invoice

The prior review fixes the denominator at **10k / 100k / 1m completed adult Travels per 30 days**, 30-minute active duration, one quote and one dispatch reprice, no reoffer/cancellation, two provider events, three push intents and one receipt each. This implies about **8.3 / 83.3 / 833** peak concurrently active Travels, **40k / 400k / 4m** local-road route requests, **20k / 200k / 2m** HERE calls if both normally routed price attempts reach HERE, and **1.2m / 12m / 120m** active telemetry writes monthly. The illustrative $20 fare + $2.50 fee, no toll/authority fee, yields $22.50 paid, $19.80 Operator fare share and **$1.08 modeled contribution before fixed insurance, staffing, legal, claims and acquisition**. Do not use it as profit or capital adequacy.

An earlier €4,648.35 quote for 1m HERE calls was corrected: published illustrative tiers yield **€3,904.35 for the first 1m**, with any second million requiring a negotiated quote. The former **40-event/minute** queue cap is removed in source: due-indexed pages of up to 200 drain up to 1,200 events per 25-second tick with eight concurrent fenced claims, backoff, dead letters and queue-age/saturation telemetry. The former **500-active-Travel** cap is removed in source: every active record is fetched in 200-row ordered pages, and congestion comparison now uses a local spatial grid. Deterministic fakes passed with 330 due events and 900 concurrently active Travels, above the inherited ~833 peak estimate. This is **not** a live 1m/month, 10× burst, SLA, provider quota or invoice test; due index build, paid provider limits, Firestore reads, host CPU and horizontal leases remain to be measured. Unit rates, direct provider bills, FX and conversions are unknown.

## Required controls before commercial use

Obtain a signed provider register with contract/SLA/quota, privacy/DPA/retention, owner, failure alarm, tested fallback and metered spend cap per market. Measure actual quote-to-book rates, duplicate/refund events, queue age, geo reads, routing/toll calls, screening cost per Operator, device push receipts, hosting load and staffed exception time. Independently bind insurance, permit/tariff, treasury float and safety staffing; do not infer them from SDK installation or mock-backed tests.
