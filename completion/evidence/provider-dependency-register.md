**Evidence class:** source architecture plus the prior [pinned-SHA architecture review](production-architecture-review.md), not contracts, invoices, live quotas or provider-console verification. Candidate integration remains a **draft PR** and no production provider was exercised for this dossier. The [Completion Ledger](../ledger.json) owns finding-level release states.

**Alternatives not adopted:** managed PostGIS/Redis may ease geo/presence scale; Onfleet/Shippo/EasyPost are merely courier candidates, not integrated capabilities; a managed case/on-call tool might help human response but does not supply staff. Do not bill unintegrated alternatives as the current architecture.

## Common monthly demand scenario — inherited, not an invoice

The prior review fixes the denominator at **10k / 100k / 1m completed adult Travels per 30 days**, 30-minute active duration, one quote and one dispatch reprice, no reoffer/cancellation, two provider events, three push intents and one receipt each. This implies about **8.3 / 83.3 / 833** peak concurrently active Travels, **40k / 400k / 4m** local-road route requests, **20k / 200k / 2m** HERE calls if both normally routed price attempts reach HERE, and **1.2m / 12m / 120m** active telemetry writes monthly. The illustrative $20 fare + $2.50 fee, no toll/authority fee, yields $22.50 paid, $19.80 Operator fare share and **$1.08 modeled contribution before fixed insurance, staffing, legal, claims and acquisition**. Do not use it as profit or capital adequacy.

An earlier €4,648.35 quote for 1m HERE calls was corrected: published illustrative tiers yield **€3,904.35 for the first 1m**, with any second million requiring a negotiated quote. The updated queue avoids starvation but still has a **40-event/minute ceiling**: at 1m completions/month × two events, average demand exceeds 40/min. The **500-Travel** monitoring scan cap is below the modeled ~833 peak; it reports overflow but does not monitor everybody. Neither national scale nor a 1m/month business is verified. Unit rates, direct provider bills, FX and conversions are unknown.

## Required controls before commercial use

Obtain a signed provider register with contract/SLA/quota, privacy/DPA/retention, owner, failure alarm, tested fallback and metered spend cap per market. Measure actual quote-to-book rates, duplicate/refund events, queue age, geo reads, routing/toll calls, screening cost per Operator, device push receipts, hosting load and staffed exception time. Independently bind insurance, permit/tariff, treasury float and safety staffing; do not infer them from SDK installation or mock-backed tests.
