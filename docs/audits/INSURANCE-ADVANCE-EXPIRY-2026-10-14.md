## Verified original gap
`backend/matching.js` rejected known expired insurance at offer time but had no advance cutoff. `backend/qualification.js` checked date expiry at the present instant; `backend/eligibility.js` reassessed on accepting, without receiving the server-computed route duration. `backend/server.js` computed `priced.minutes` but did not persist it in each prepared Travel. Therefore a Travel could begin shortly before a known policy expiry with too little remaining validity.

## Minimal candidate change
- Add `backend/insurance-horizon.js`: one strict UTC-validated date-only end timestamp and policy for **minimum 24h remaining** or **server-routed Travel duration plus 1h**, whichever is greater. Treat missing, invalid or unreliable duration/time as insufficient evidence (except unestimated duration receives the conservative minimum). Date-only UTC cutoff is deliberately earlier than South Florida local end-of-day; it does not extend a policy.
- Wire at `matchOperator` for all newly offered Travels, at `assessOperator` during duty and acceptance using the independently verified document, plus the fleet copy at acceptance. Pass server-priced route minutes through the prepared ride to atomically recheck during assignment and acceptance. Zero additional database reads; existing transaction retries continue.
- Automatic restoration uses existing independently verified new policy approval and source confirmation. Warned Operators can use existing `/operator/insurance` flow, not a new portal. No automatic cancellation of already accepted rides or stopping passenger safety support.
- Documented operational lockout is **not** an interpretation that Florida law has been nullified or that non-notified retroactive cancellations are impossible. Source-based external legal and insurance obligations remain separate.
