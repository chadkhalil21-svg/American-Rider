## Authorities researched before decision

1. [Florida's 2026 TNC statute, §627.748(7)(a)-(h) and (8)(d)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0627/Sections/0627.748.html). Driver or TNC may provide required qualifying coverage for the logged-on and engaged periods. In (7)(d), *when the driver coverage lapses or lacks required coverage*, coverage maintained by the TNC **must** respond from first dollar and defend. A platform lockout is a useful prevention control, **not an express statutory substitute**. A prearranged Travel is defined from acceptance until last passenger exit; a claim and private-driving determination require actual contemporaneous evidence.
2. [Firebase/Google Cloud Firestore best practices](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/best-practices) and [reads/writes at scale](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/understand-reads-writes-scale): bounded indexed/cursor reads, avoid repeat scans, no hot documents, 500/50/5 progressive ramp when appropriate. Count billed reads and index entry reads against production usage; do not infer costs from empty/synthetic datasets.
3. [AWS Builders' Library — making retries safe with idempotent APIs](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/) and [limit retries](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/rel_mitigate_interaction_failure_limit_retries.html): replay payment and provider messages without duplicate side effects, cap and jitter retries and measure backlogs.
4. [NASA software engineering verification/validation, NPR 7150.2C](https://nodis3.gsfc.nasa.gov/displayAll.cfm?Internal_ID=N_PR_7150_002C_) and [SWE-055](https://swehb.nasa.gov/spaces/SWEHBVD/pages/102695440/SWE-055%2B-%2BRequirements%2BValidation): an automated mock verifies code assumptions, not device, emergency staffing, financial counterparty, law or intended environment.
5. [Stripe test environment](https://docs.stripe.com/testing) and [Connect testing](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/testing): test mode can simulate declines, asynchronous refunds, chargebacks, Connect transfers and retries without live funds; do **not** stress-test Stripe test mode. [Separate charges/transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers) require independent tracking of charges, transfers, refunds and reversals.
6. [Apple design principles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles/), [Apple accessible feedback](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/feedback), [Nielsen Norman Group visibility](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visibility-system-status/), [Stanford d.school](https://dschool.stanford.edu/tools/design-thinking-bootleg), [IDEO](https://designthinking.ideo.com/process), [MIT Sloan platform strategy](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/platform-strategy-explained), and [Harvard Business School service design](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?research=7002): prevent errors, show legible reasons and next actions, measure both-sided service outcomes and economical operation. ## Go/no-go acceptance and Council reading

- **Release gate GREEN only when:** (1) legal/insurer evidence satisfactory for Florida, including `company_insurance_bound`; (2) provider-side tagged Stripe sandbox money lifecycle with independent IDs, then small authorized live-money proof; (3) physical iOS+Android critical paths and truly received emergency alerts; (4) measured Firestore and Render capacity under representative staging demand, with quota, p95 latency and reliable recovery; (5) genuine Operator carrier proof, screening, facility permissions where applicable, and active region eligibility; and (6) whole-screen accessible bilingual/multilingual interaction audit with no high-severity defects. Each sign-off is tied to exact code SHA and independent observed evidence.
- **No unearned certification:** a successful test suite cannot imply a carrier is bound, a claim is defended, an emergency worker responds, or a new market is commercially authorized. Unknown state fails closed. Record exception owner, evidence link and recommended first action. Do not add recurring paid vendors or founder-operated manual queues without a measured case.
- **Council synthesis**: Ford—one reusable national policy+case engine, not market-by-market scripts. Rockefeller—limit vendor calls, bound Firebase reads and price using measured contribution. Jobs/Apple—one simple status and clear remedy; show an occupied Traveler continuity rather than terminating an already-started safety journey in the UI. MIT/HBS/Stanford—test supply availability, fair Operator economics, true consumer outcomes, not vanity signups. NASA/Amazon/Google—prove real behavior under failure, exact deployment and intended conditions.
