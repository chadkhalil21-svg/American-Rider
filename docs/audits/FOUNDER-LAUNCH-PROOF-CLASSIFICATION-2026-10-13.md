## Concrete software correction and rules of proof
This change classifies **absence of an attestation record** (`record_absent`), **valid but stale-version proof** (`record_version_mismatch`), **invalid provenance or future review date** (`record_invalid`), and **expired evidence** (`record_expired`) separately. Missing production verification is `production_verification_missing`. The live founder command logs these status codes without policy identifiers, personal reports, credentials or document text. A missing record is **not** called an unfinished test. All original transaction, insurer, screening, device, permit, payment and per-county commercial safeguards remain operative; there is NO new activation exemption. New two-pass tests simulate absent/present/expired/future-dated proofs and unchanged hard blocking. Approval remains conditional on actual evidence, not source-control comments.
