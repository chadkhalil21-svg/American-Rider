# American Rider — national competitive fare positioning: research-only release audit
**Decision date:** 2026-10-12 local / 2026-10-13 UTC. **Status:** RESEARCH AND REGRESSION ONLY. **No passenger fare, market promotion, source subscription, insurance commitment, admission or Stripe change.**

## Founder decision — correct the economic objective
American Rider seeks a **competitive ordinary price**, rather than racing as far below market as possible. The business preference is **around 90% of the actual typical comparable local Traveler Total**, with tolerance for 85–100% where warranted by cost, customer value and measured conversion. These are decision preferences, not automatically guaranteed discounts, absolute caps or independent facts. Raising an unnecessarily cheap price may improve Operator earnings and platform sustainability. Customer trust, demand, geographic availability, routing and pickup time also affect competitiveness.

The system is NATIONAL by design, with Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach commissioned first. Never use a national pooled median as the rate for a given home-to-zoo Travel; never give Texas Florida's coefficients. Never imply the founders' Council or named institutions individually advised or approved a particular price.

## What the server does today
`backend/fareauthority.js`: selects the actual road route when available, computes routed miles and time from the region-specific `backend/market-pricing.js` record, resolves tolls/regulated fees, and adds `backend/economics.js`'s minimum sufficient Platform Fee to produce **one Traveler Total**. For South Florida only:
- Standard Travel Fare = max($3, $1 + $0.85/routed mile + $0.15/routed minute).
- Platform Fee >= $2.50; model currently requires >= $0.75 per-Travel modeled contribution AFTER modeled fees, $0.25 risk reserve and $0.25 operating allowance; the earlier founder conversation discussed a different $1.25 margin, **but current ratified `docs/FARE-MODEL.md` says $0.75**. Reconcile the decision and actual corporate regulatory/insurance cost before claiming full actual profitability.
- Operator receives 99% of Travel Fare plus toll reimbursement; separately charged $2 active Operator fee waived on 20 completed Travels/month.
- `market-reference-authority.js` requires a promoted, independently held-out, route/time/class-matched full-Total local market cell, source diversity and valid economics before using any reference. `market-reference-fare.js` then raises an underpriced current Fare **towards** the 90% target. It never lowers the existing production Fare. This matches the founder's *avoid unnecessary underpricing* intent, but must be evaluated for actual overpriced cases rather than represented as an absolute 10% discount.
- No live rights-approved ordinary South Florida quote feed exists. Published government taxi tariffs, worldwide pooled medians, informal models and event price surges are **not** a substitute. No direct provider API lookup per Travel is needed or permitted until contracted.

## Exactly what a home-to-zoo Travel needs
Example only; arbitrary **synthetic** 5mi/20min with no pass-through:
- Our configured Standard Travel Fare $8.25; floor Platform Fee $2.50; **$10.75 Traveler Total**. US card basis, no extras.
- A hypothetical separately verified, same-class/time/local ordinary comparable market total of **$14.00** would imply ~**$12.60** desired all-in (90%). The existing backend fare solver can raise our Fare to approximately the desired Total while preserving its economic contribution and 99% share. No outside API request for each traveler.
- A hypothetical market at $9.00 does NOT authorize reducing American Rider's $10.75 baseline. That requires a separate company strategy and Operator economics decision, not the 90% preference alone.
- If roads cannot be routed accurately or airport/port/tolls are uncertain, do not pass an invented $0 through or substitute a published flat median. Follow existing authoritative routing and fail-closed gates.
- **At a national scale** the approved route-class/market-daypart benchmark is cached; a supplier is checked **only at its authorized revision cadence**, source fingerprints prevent rewriting identical observations and conditional promotion follows holdout/consent/expiry rules. No per-Travel paid supplier polling.

Current modeled formula yields these no-toll Standard passenger Totals (USD, US-issued card; research *not* live observed prices):

| Simulated routed route | Travel Fare | Platform Fee | Traveler Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 miles / 10 minutes | $5.05 | $2.50 | $7.55 |
| 5 miles / 20 minutes | $8.25 | $2.50 | $10.75 |
| 8 miles / 21 minutes | $10.95 | $2.50 | $13.45 |
| 10 miles / 25 minutes | $13.25 | $2.50 | $15.75 |

Note published RideWise Miami 5mi model $12.41–15.79 is **above** our $10.75; 10mi model $18.66–23.74 is **above** our $15.75. This warrants cross-validation and a *candidate* raised coefficient set once reliable sample data arrives, NOT an immediate production repricing based on unverified modeled assumptions. An older September 2026 single-route company benchmark had lower contemporaneous offers than these publications; without identical-route evidence there is no certified uplift.

## National rollout and promotion gates
1. Use free published market-level statistics only to identify obvious outliers and prioritize routes to audit. Cite date and geographical scope. Never promote them as individual route prices.
2. Use 4–6 local route-length bands × morning/midday/evening × weekday/weekend × standard class, plus airport/regulated categories. Sample real ordinary ALL-IN offered Totals from multiple independent, rights-authorized sources; anonymize, exclude tips/personalized offers/event surge. FIPS/metro group licensed where valid; no rural inference from another state.
3. Compare full Totals to server quotes for precisely the same road route, time, tolls and currency. Track median, confidence bounds, 10th/90th percentile, sample age, acceptance, cancellation, ETA, driver pickup/deadhead and hourly realized pay. Per-cell >=20 observations and multiple family/provider constraints are MINIMUMS, not a precision guarantee.
4. Refit **market-specific** base/mile/minute candidate coefficients to avoid being unnecessarily cheap across short, medium and long Travels. Avoid one global multiplier because the mix and fare floors differ. Preserve 99% of fare, operator duty-time pay, legal costs, platform fee and one Total. Choose a rate that represents value and avoids 20–30% *unnecessary* discounts, not mandatory 90% on every trip.
5. Hold out separate observations and test matched route diversity, outage/duplicates/revisions and 10k/100k/1m simulated operations. Release as a versioned, rollback-able candidate only when evidence fits, independent legal/insurance/settlement gates are not weakened. No literal Council endorsement.
6. Watch after release: observed Traveler conversion, Operator availability/acceptance, wait times, refunds and platform contribution. Revise only on genuine signal, with guarded cadence and reversibility; do not react to one volatile quote.
7. Provider B2B rights and pricing not yet granted. America Rider Communications already contacted Obi (including referred Diane) and Gridwise on Oct 11/12 ET. This document does **not** buy anything, authorize a competitive API, or open a market.
