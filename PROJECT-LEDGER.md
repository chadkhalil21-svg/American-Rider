## 2. Verified software — do not build it again

**Onboarding / insurance**
- `backend/markets.js` + `backend/regions.js` determine county and jurisdiction using official Florida county geometry and a single `fl-southeast` region for Miami-Dade/Broward/Palm Beach. `backend/jurisdictions/us.json` lists 50 states, DC and five territories; identity records are not an operating license.
- `backend/insurance-jurisdictions.js` implements Florida's Operator coverage requirements, including logged-on 50/100/25 thousand, $1m during a ride and PIP. Unknown state insurance requirements are not silently copied from Florida.
- `backend/qualification.js` recomputes status on every relevant qualification/duty/acceptance path. It checks documents, insured and vehicle identity, policy period and TNC use, minimum limits, screening, suspension, payout readiness and disclosure. `backend/matching.js`/server gates also reject lapse and stale presence. No saved "approved" flag is permanent authority.
- `backend/insurance-monitoring.js`: independent *first* status confirmation (an uploaded declaration alone is not proof); 30-day refresh, bounded 5-day attestation grace, adverse events immediately reject, 250-record rotating cursor. `backend/insurance-contact-trust.js` + `backend/insurance-official-source-router.js` check provider contact identity; Florida DFS business-license email exact match is **contact evidence only**, not proof of a live policy. `backend/insurance-reminders.test.js` covers bounded notification retries; PR #84 deployed.
