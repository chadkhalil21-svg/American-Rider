## External evidence and researched Council criteria
- **Official Florida 2026 §627.728(3)(a) and §627.7281**: nonpayment cancellation of covered motor vehicle policies generally requires at least ten days' notice to specified recipients. Some premium-financed arrangements have different provisions. Notices directed to the insured and agent are NOT automatically delivered to our platform, so programming a ten-day alert without a source is insufficient. https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/627.728 ; https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/627.7281
- **Official Florida 2026 §627.748(7)**: the TNC driver or owner can maintain qualifying primary insurance. Separate TNC-maintained coverage obligations if driver coverage fails remain an external statutory question; neither advance warnings nor this patch guarantee how claims are treated. https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/627.748
- **Google SRE** favors automation of repetitive toil and meaningful exception-only alerts; **Firebase** recommends bounded indexed reads, not full-fleet polling. Reuse our existing monitoring and per-Operator verification; no additional periodic full-fleet scan. https://sre.google/workbook/eliminating-toil/ ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/best-practices
- **Amazon Builders' Library** recommends retry safety; here repeat notices are idempotently constrained to one adverse state, a transaction and existing audit framework. A historic confirmation must not clear a new cancellation report. https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-apis/
- **NASA SWE-055** calls for testing normal and adverse conditions in the intended environment. A simulation cannot prove insurer feed coverage, live mobile permission behavior, actual bank payment status or real-world claim exposure. https://swehb.nasa.gov/spaces/SWEHBVD/pages/102695440/SWE-055%2B-%2BRequirements%2BValidation
- **Council application (rubric, not an endorsement):** Ford: repeatable one-path decision; Rockefeller: no new costly vendor as a prerequisite; Jobs/Apple: simple Operator choices; Harvard/Stanford/MIT: test measurable outcomes; first-class service: precise private instructions, no claims about knowing their premium transactions.
