## Pre-deployment controls

1. Protect `release/current`. Keep PR #59 draft. Review the entire diff and inventory, independently approve the exact source commit, pinned lockfiles and licenses. Run check-only CI on that SHA and record failures; never treat a prior commit's green badge as the final SHA.
2. Provision environment-scoped secrets **outside Git** through approved systems: Firebase Admin, Stripe server/webhook/Connect, HERE, R2, Resend, Expo/APNs/FCM, region-specific OTP feed, screening provider and retained Anthropic support/monitoring key. `TEEN_PIN_SECRET`, named `OPS_USERS` TOTP secrets and independent `OPS_SESSION_SECRET` are required for those protected paths; do not rotate the Teen secret while an active Teen Travel depends on it. Twilio is not a mobile feature until native SDK/credential/device proof exists.
3. Deploy the exact Firestore security rules and composite indexes **before** enabling scheduled, Family and provider-queue workers; verify index build status and actual query results. Demonstrate network isolation, privacy logs, least privilege, staff MFA and limited retention.
4. Establish source-of-truth accounting per PaymentIntent, charge, refund, transfer, payout and bank settlement. A pending or processing refund is a debt, not success. Reconcile orphan charges and charged-but-unmatched Travels daily; record idempotency keys, actual balances, fee/toll payees, dispute reserves and owner sign-off.
5. Name staffed safety and financial exception rota; rehearse alert delivery failure, offline device, underage/custody dispute, inaccessible pickup, accident, service animal, discrimination complaint, carrier lapse and cancellation after payment. The emergency screen offers **device dialing 911**; neither a case nor a push call dispatches help by itself.
6. Rehearse account closure while a PaymentIntent is preparing, a paid Travel is unmatched, an Operator is offered and a scheduled off-session charge is dispatching. The new server-only `account_closures/{uid}` tombstone transactionally prevents new bookings/scheduled reservations and is denied to mobile clients; reserved scheduled documents are deleted only while their claim remains reserved. The app still deletes the Firestore profile and Firebase Auth in **two distinct steps**; a failed late Auth deletion can leave an active login with no profile and a closing tombstone. A named privacy/engineering owner must define and test retry/recovery, regulated record retention and tombstone deletion, including real Auth/Firestore failures. **Not launch-certified** by the local fake-Firestore regressions.

## County activation is a two-stage institutional decision

1. `GET /ops/markets` is a named, MFA-authenticated Operations checklist. Every configured county begins effectively **waitlist**, even if source geography labels it active. `GET /markets` shows the effective public state; unauthorised counties receive no paid fare/route/booking, screening, document-OCR or Connect onboarding. Register interest cheaply without retaining **precise** coordinates; an out-of-county record keeps only a half-degree approximate area cell. Server-only `market_admission` evidence and audit collections are denied by default to mobile clients.
2. Before any paid Operator intake, independently inspect and record seven current core records: jurisdiction authority, bound company coverage, Operator coverage process, screening contract, disclosure/retention plan, provider contracts and staffed safety plan. All source-region conditions and the essential live provider/admin/Ops conditions must pass. Then a named Ops user may click **Authorize prelaunch Operator intake**. This enables only qualification, screening, document review and Connect for that county; it **does not** admit a passenger booking, off-session charge, Operator duty or offer. Physical accessibility and qualified insured supply must now be demonstrated, not presumed. No prelaunch authorization has been made in this task.
3. Record the remaining independent requirements—qualified accessible fleet, pricing/tax and facility controls where applicable, staffed service, tested payment reconciliation, backup/restore drill, native build/device and release proof—against the same manifest version and expiry dates. **Every proof is bound to the exact manifest reviewed by named Ops.** A source/jurisdiction/policy change makes all prior-version proofs insufficient; the Ops evidence form rejects a stale displayed version. Changing evidence pauses the market and queues on-duty fleet cleanup, not auto-approves it. After cleanup and a separate business/insurer/legal and finance review, a named Ops user may click **Activate county**; the server rechecks all live requirements transactionally and audits the transition. **The presence of a button is not independent approval.** No county has been activated by this source change.
4. On expiry, changed source version, missing provider prerequisites, insurer/permit lapse or human stop instruction, **Pause intake and new offers**. The paid assignment and final Operator on-duty transactions both read the same durable admission document as the pause. The pause response reports whether bounded fleet shutdown is still pending; the leased cleanup resumes through on-duty fleet pages, including older GPS-only records. **Do not reactivate until the fleet cleanup marker clears** and independently check the actual Operator console/devices. If an index/query/write fails, the marker remains and its alarm must be staffed; no paid offers can proceed. Scheduled and reoffer sweeps also check current admission before new work. Already-running Travel, emergency filing, cancellations and refunds stay available. A pending PaymentIntent created before a pause can still complete later; treat it as an unmatched charge and reconcile/refund, never assume a Git rollback reverses it. Prelaunch intake can be paused separately without claiming public service ended.
5. The new checklist and deterministic fake-Firestore tests are **source evidence only**. Firestore index/rules deploy, authentic Ops MFA session, independently verified external records, real Stripe/CRA/provider test runs, native devices, actual duty coverage and per-county sign-off remain NOT TESTED. Do not call the fallback adult South Florida release live simply because software now has a county switch.

Traveler Home checks a 15-second-cached, no-geocoding `POST /markets/at` for a real selected pickup before showing new on-demand or scheduled sales. The endpoint reads only the pickup county rather than all national markets, and exact coordinates stay in the short-lived request body rather than a logged query URL. A merely prelaunch/waitlist county gets a consent-based interest control, not an Operator offer; a database/network outage gets a retry state. Existing Travel, receipts and help remain reachable. Rehearse actual iOS/Android authenticated Home, location denial/manual pickup, boundary movement, privacy notice and a real waitlist contact path before claiming national acquisition readiness; local source checks alone do not certify those journeys.

## Real-world acceptance campaign, after written authorization

Use low-value staged charge test accounts or an expressly approved live pilot and qualified insured staff. Retain case/travel/Stripe/refund IDs and timestamped logs; no personal secrets in the dossier. For each intended market and app platform:

- On-demand: quote both endpoints/permits/tolls, confirm a server-priced amount, take PaymentSheet result, independently verify provider success, reserve exactly one Operator, accept/decline/reoffer **the same** Travel, arrive/board/complete, receipt/payout and bank match. Simulate lost HTTP response after provider success, simultaneous two-Travel offer, lost phone, stale location, revoked user, missing provider and no Operator; prove refunds reach terminal provider/bank state.
- Scheduled: compare booking timestamp/quote to dispatch-time route/toll, off-session payment mandate, due-ordered Firestore index, overlapping worker leases, card decline, delayed success, abandoned prepared booking, provider webhook failure, recovery sweep, cancellation/refund, Guardian permission and staff handling.
- Family/Teen: test an authorized/unauthorized Guardian, revoked link during offer, PIN retrieval without push, lockout, no replay, custody/ID handoff, lost phone and practical child-safety response. Carrier/legal sign-off is separate from source test results.
- Smart Travel: test actual regional OTP and licensed GTFS/RT, fare-unknown display, both paid car legs, disrupted transfer, wrong stop, fee alignment, restart and cancellation. Agency fare is separate from American Rider charges.
- Return, Android, national markets, airport/port, native calling, Courier and recurring series each require their own Ledger release-later evidence; do not let a passing adult ride silently turn them on. National **search** is not national permission to sell.
- Repeat accessible flows with VoiceOver/TalkBack, large text, offline conditions, language switching and real native background/notification permissions. A screenshot of the unauthenticated web shell is not an authenticated or native test.

## Monitoring, incident and recovery

- Monitor queue age, due counts, failed/dead provider events, paid/unassigned Travel age, refund pending age, stale Operator assignments, monitoring overflow, 5xx/provider timeouts and offline alert receipts. Stripe disputes, charge failures and payout exceptions now require both a durable idempotent case and an accepted alert; restricted Operator accounts require a successful fleet write before the queued event is done. The new `GET /ops/provider-events/dead` returns bounded metadata; named MFA Operations may `POST /ops/provider-events/:id/replay` only after checking provider state/ownership. The replay is audited and **may duplicate side effects if the handler is not idempotent**; never blindly replay money or emergency notifications. An accepted email is not a human response; require staffed acknowledgement, evidence deadline ownership and debt reconciliation.
- On signed dispute creation, a create-only SHA-256-fingerprinted packet of narrowly selected Travel/payment facts is saved under `dispute_evidence/{disputeId}`; `/ops/disputes` shows it to named Operations in bounded phone-friendly pages. This **does not submit** evidence to Stripe or decide whether to contest. Confirm the actual Stripe deadline, original records, missing Travel match, provider history, privacy/retention and legal hold before any human-approved financial submission. The existing Operations board now shows bounded exception samples rather than reading every historical Travel; a “+” is a lower bound, and its recent-sample dollar values are **not** accounting totals. When a section saturates, staff must use direct case/provider reconciliation; do not infer there are no older urgent cases.
- Named MFA Operations can inspect 40 oldest-open cases per page at `/ops/cases?kind=emergency` and `/ops/cases?kind=support`. A named person records a note of at least 12 characters to acknowledge the case; closure is a separate noted action requiring prior acknowledgement, each audit-written in the same transaction as the case state. Payment disputes retain their existing urgent case identity for retry compatibility but show **PAYMENT DEADLINE**, not a physical safety emergency, on the page and in new alert email subjects. Check **every** page; do not mistake an acknowledgement click for calling 911, contacting the claimant or meeting Stripe's evidence deadline. Live MFA, queue index, staffed contacts, escalation and actual human response remain untested external release requirements.
- Keep the privileged Ops and safety duty roster current. For a reported emergency: prioritize caller's direct 911 action, timestamp the case, page staffed safety, verify someone acknowledged, preserve route/location/vehicle evidence and initiate insurer/regulator notice as required; do not infer human acknowledgement from an accepted email/push.
- Configure and rehearse Firestore scheduled backups/PITR restoration into a separate database, index/rule reapplication and an R2 document-restore/delete drill. Verify restored Auth/ride/provider-event references and bank ledger against Stripe. Firebase backup/PITR is an available product feature, **not evidence it has been enabled**.
- If a source/deploy mismatch, unexplained funds, uninsured Operator, failed screening, permit gap or unstaffed safety desk appears: freeze new offers in the affected market, preserve pending refunds and unaltered audit records, keep emergency access and already-running Travel support, page the responsible humans and roll back only to a known signed SHA after invariant checks. A Git revert alone does not reverse Stripe or Firestore transactions.

## Expansion and stop criteria

At 10k/100k/1m completed adult Travels per month, gather actual p95/p99, saturation metrics and per-Travel provider bills before raising load. The old 40-event queue and 500-Travel monitoring ceilings have been replaced in source by due-indexed bounded multi-page event draining and complete paged active-Travel scans; deterministic local 330-event/900-active fixtures pass, **not** an actual national or sustained-load test. Require deployed indexes, delayed-provider/retry-storm and multi-instance lease drills, realistic 10× peak, staff alarm response and measured cost before claiming 1m/month. Scheduled due ordering alone cannot certify nationwide capacity. Approve additional market packets individually (insurer, law, accessible fleet, tariff, facility, staffing, provider quotas, rollback). **Founder approval before any merge into `release/current` is still mandatory.**
