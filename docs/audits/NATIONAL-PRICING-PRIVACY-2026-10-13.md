# National pricing evidence and opt-in privacy controls — October 13, 2026

## Founder decision / Council framework
One national pricing architecture, one set of price-integrity rules; each **specific admitted county/market** owns its own lawful, temporally relevant evidence, regulatory economics, independent holdouts and local reference cells. Founder target is **competitive Traveler pricing** while protecting the 99% Operator fare share and expenses; **not an arbitrary universal discount**. Council references are published research/design disciplines, not actual consultation with deceased historical business leaders.

### External sources that led the changes
- [Google Firebase Firestore TTL and index definition](https://firebase.google.com/docs/reference/firestore/indexes) confirms `fieldOverrides[].ttl=true` with `indexes=[]`. [Google Firestore TTL documentation](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/ttl) confirms deletion is not instantaneous, often within 24 hours after expiry, and deletion operations incur costs. A committed declaration does **not** establish that Firebase has deployed and activated the TTL policy.
- [FTC mobile app guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/marketing-your-mobile-app-get-it-right-start): notice, affirmative agreement for unexpected use, minimization, easy choices and honoring promises. We therefore reject passive `useFocusEffect` price-view tracking; consent must be an actual optional user gesture, in all five app languages.
- [Amazon Builders Library idempotent APIs](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/): reuse the exact random signed event identity; do not double-count retry or change the event meaning. The feature is still isolated from authoritative booking transactions and payments.
- [NASA SWE-055](https://swehb.nasa.gov/spaces/SWEHBVD/pages/102695440/SWE-055%2B-%2BRequirements%2BValidation): synthetic tests cover opt-out, forged consent request, stale token, changed route, overlimit report, unconfigured TTL and another market; physical device, actual Firebase policy and real data conditions require separate commissioning.
- [Chicago City 2026 TNP trips](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Transportation/Transportation-Network-Providers-Trips-2026-/8bbv-3wbe): records contain Fare, rounded Tip, Additional Charges and Trip Total; these are not guaranteed to be a comparable, untipped *upfront* consumer quote. We now use the current 2026 dataset and actual `trip_total`, but mark it **structural/audit evidence only** until a valid full-Total normalization exists; never market-price authority alone.
- [NYC TLC March 2025 HVFHV dictionary](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/tlc/downloads/pdf/data_dictionary_trip_records_hvfhs.pdf): `base_passenger_fare` is **before** tolls, taxes, tips and fees. It is not a current all-in Traveler Total. An analytical record has a passenger-charge field without thereby establishing usable fare-comparison evidence.
- [Stanford GSB marketplace experiment interference research](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/interference-bias-variance-two-sided-marketplace-experimentation): price changes affect two-sided availability, so descriptive booking study ≠ causal price experiment. Protect both Operators and Travelers.

## Actual repository state
`backend/market-evidence.js` already has nationwide source types, separate market plans and no default; `backend/market-reference-authority.js` requires matching distance/time/daypart, 20+ observations, independent evidence families, concentration <=70%, verified comparable all-in totals and approved holdout/economics/router snapshots. `backend/market-pricing.js` has **only Florida Southeast production pricing**; New York, Chicago, Texas, California, etc. do **not** inherit Florida rates or automatically become operating markets. This is correct national fail-closed behavior.

`backend/quote-view-store.js` already deletes associated account research in resumable bounded batches and records seven-day expiries but had no Firestore TTL declaration; passive focused confirmation automatically submitted views if server flags were enabled. Its enabled conditions remained false in the repo. The privacy policy did not describe voluntary quoted-price analysis in all languages.

## Changes under test
1. Declare `pricing_quote_views.expiresAt` TTL in `firestore.indexes.json`, exempting that monotonic field from unnecessary indexing. This still requires **actual Firebase index deployment and verification** before setting `PRICE_VIEWS_TTL_READY=1`.
2. Remove passive quote-view posting and add a truly optional, untranslated-state-free checkbox at the price-confirmation footer, only visible when the otherwise OFF server research pilot issues a token. Share nothing unless the Traveler taps. Backend accepts only `consentToResearch===true` and the current `quote-study-v1` notice version, and stores timestamp/version. No change to single Total or booking acceptance.
3. Draft and publish accurate optional research disclosures in English, Spanish, French, Italian and German. The core legal documents remain draft and should be reviewed by competent counsel; engineering changes or this document cannot legally certify a nationwide privacy practice.
4. Correct public source categorization: observed fare/base passenger fare is **not** an independently matched full upfront Traveler Total. Chicago and NYC remain useful for historic route length, travel-time distribution and demand. NYC base passenger fare is truthfully tagged as *partial* charge.
5. Two-pass national-source, privacy and index-declaration regressions in synthetic-only CI.

## Deployment, cost and activation gates
- The pilot should cost **zero Firestore research reads/writes** when disabled. When enabled after validation, each affirmatively submitted view creates at most one idempotent research record and optional prepared booking causes one separate bounded transaction; manual /ops report caps at 120 reads. TTL deletes are billable; 10K, 100K and 1M are stress/cost scenarios, not proof of measured live costs or sample representativeness.
- Before enabling: inspect **actual Firebase project** TTL policy status, deploy indexes with project-specific IAM only, verify account deletion/withdrawal and index queries with synthetic emulator and genuine device consent/refresh; confirm localized notices and legally appropriate lawful basis/privacy rights. **Do not set Render's four pilot flags or user-visible claims of activation until these prerequisites pass.**
- After lawful pilot, own opt-in displayed quotes and prepared/paid/completed booking outcomes can inform controlled local experiments, not self-certifying independent market prices.
- Coverage rollout: regional public datasets and verified academic/local sources for structure; licensed or user-voluntary rights-permitted *real route/time/full Total* comparisons in each activated market; no competitor account automation or scraping without express permission; no transposing Chicago/NYC historic prices onto Miami/Texas. Where local sources do not establish independence, freshness, full total and minimum sample, the current explicit local formula applies **only to legally admitted markets with separately approved coefficients**. Others fail closed and stay on waitlist until commissioned.

## Incomplete by design
This PR does not connect Firebase admin privileges, activate live paid data collection, buy a national supplier feed, prove independent market prices for every US county, resolve legacy voluntary-market-contributor privacy deletion issue #169, or commission 50 states as transport networks. Flagging those distinctions protects the founder and users against false readiness statements.
