# American Rider — Your Roadmap (Plain English)

No jargon. Here's the whole thing, in order, and — most importantly — **what YOU personally
need to do at each stage vs. what gets handled for you.** Keep this. Read it whenever the
"wait, how does all this work??" feeling comes back.

The big idea to hold onto: **you don't do it all at once, and you don't do it alone.**
Your job is the vision, talking to drivers and riders, and making decisions. The plumbing
gets handed to tools, partners, a lawyer, and engineers.

---

## ✅ Stage 0 — Where you are RIGHT NOW (the hard part to start — basically done)

The demo. The rider app, the driver app, Smart Travel, the maps, the whole look and feel.
This is what shows people the vision. **Most people never get this far. You're past it.**

- **What YOU do:** show it to people. Drivers, riders, your friend. Collect reactions.
- **Handled for you:** the building (me).
- **Cost:** basically free.

---

## 🟢 Stage 1 — A tiny real pilot in Miami (the first "real" step)

The goal is NOT to build the whole national platform. It's to do **a handful of real rides
with people you already know**, and prove it works. This needs a stripped-down real version —
not all the screens, no AI yet. Just: sign in, see the map, book a ride, pay, driver gets paid.

- **What YOU do:**
  - Line up **2–3 drivers** you've already talked to.
  - Start their **background check + commercial insurance early** — those take days-to-weeks, so begin before everything else is ready.
  - Round up **a few riders** from your own circle.
  - Open a **Stripe account** (Stripe = the company that moves the money). Business account.
  - Decide **DBA vs. LLC** (an LLC protects your other stuff once real money moves — recommended).
- **Handled for you:** the actual build (an engineer, or me), Stripe (payments), the background-check vendor.
- **The honest part:** this is the "get a little help" phase — a bit of money **or** a technical partner so you're not carrying the build alone. Weeks, not months, for a stripped pilot.

---

## 🔧 Stage 2 — Make it solid + a real Miami launch

Now you harden it and open it to strangers (not just friends).

- Add the polish, real support (the instant AI help flow), and a simple dashboard for **you** to watch rides.
- Automate driver onboarding (auto-check the insurance + background docs) so you can add drivers without doing each one by hand.
- **What YOU do:** sign the legal stuff, keep recruiting, run the marketing (the "drivers keep 99%" and receipt-comparison posts).
- **Handled for you:**
  - **A lawyer** — reviews your terms and the money flow, confirms Florida's rideshare registration. (This is where the "is this allowed?" questions get answered by a pro — not by you guessing.)
  - **Stripe** — keeps splitting the money automatically (rider pays once; drivers + platform each get their share).
- **The honest part:** real dev + legal spend here. This is the fundable / bring-in-help stage.

---

## 🔁 Stage 3 — Grow (more drivers, more riders, then more cities)

- Turn on the smart stuff: Revenue Roadmap, predictive traffic, and **Smart Travel's real transit tickets** (through a partner like **Token Transit / Masabi** — the same one Uber uses; they hold the deals with transit agencies, so you don't have to).
- Expand to new cities — **re-check each state's rules first** (insurance and background-check laws differ; nothing transfers automatically).
- **What YOU do:** vision, partnerships (transit agencies, Brightline), hiring, growth.

---

## What's ALWAYS your job — and what's NEVER yours to do alone

| Always yours | Never yours to do alone (delegate it) |
|---|---|
| The vision & the pitch | Writing the code → **engineers / me** |
| Talking to drivers & riders | Moving/splitting money → **Stripe** |
| Decisions & priorities | "Is this legal?" → **a lawyer** |
| Recruiting the first drivers | Transit tickets → **Token Transit / Masabi** |
| Choosing the direction | Driver insurance → **the drivers + insurers** |

If something feels over your head, that's the signal it belongs in the right-hand column.

---

## The ONE next step (don't think past this)

1. **Keep showing the demo** and collecting feedback (you're doing it).
2. **Talk to 2–3 drivers** about a small paid pilot — gauge real interest.
3. When you're ready to build the real pilot, **find a bit of funding or one technical helper** — that's the unlock for Stage 1.

That's it. Everything else waits its turn. You've already done the part that stops most
people cold — the rest is a checklist, in order, with help.
