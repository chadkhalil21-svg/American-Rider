# National market pricing evidence — release-aware, not city-hour polling

**Decision record, 2026-10-11.** Source-led review of actual `release/current`, [national operating model](NATIONAL-OPERATING-MODEL.md), [canonical ledger](../../PROJECT-LEDGER.md), and the founder's institutional Council rubric.

## Why Chicago matters nationally

Chicago's former uncommissioned collector fetched up to 1,000 historical ride records per hour; persisting them every hour could produce roughly 24,000 documents per day for ONE research city while no users were riding. PR #101 disabled research-only ingestion, and PR #103 added zero-Firestore-work for markets without commissioned collectors, source fingerprints, bounded append-only creates and no unnecessary price snapshot rewrites. The early incident proves why **market count must not multiply background work**. Do not reopen those fixes without post-fix evidence.

The earlier Chicago job was intended to learn comparative **historical trip fare distributions**, not to calculate prices at the time of a Travel request. It was not a lawful live market-wide price quote feed. Public records are historical: the [City of Chicago publisher](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/transportation-network-providers-trips-2025) reports fare rounding and a September 23, 2026 dataset update, and its [update announcement](https://data.cityofchicago.org/stories/s/Change-Notice-Transportation-Network-Provider-Data/wmdt-6h9e/) describes monthly publication and retrospective changes. [NYC TLC](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page) publishes monthly trip data with approximately a two-month lag. Querying such datasets hourly cannot make their source prices real time.

## What exists in American Rider and what does NOT

- `backend/market-evidence.js` declares jurisdiction-specific plans, source eligibility, diversity, rights and coverage. The three Florida counties have plans but **no active automated market-price evidence collectors**. `backend/market-evidence-collectors.js` includes only the Chicago research adapter.
- `backend/market-reference-service.js` skips work before obtaining a Firestore client if no commissioned collector exists. It limits a market to at most 100 candidate observation rows per sweep, uses append-only `create()`, computes source medians rather than weighting by raw record volume, and writes a candidate reference only when genuinely new observations require it. This PR additionally makes its source fingerprint invariant to record ordering and JSON property order: the same publication shuffled is not a pricing event. Tests prove equal samples, substantive changes and repeated loads.
- `backend/market-reference-authority.js` requires promoted, diverse, time-valid, cell-matched evidence before a quote can consume a price reference. `backend/pricing-policy.js` defines a **0.90 target fraction** as a market-analysis rule, **not** proof of actual current independent prices.
- `backend/market-reference-monitor.js` declares conceptual external/own-market freshness and promotion controls. Its `externalObservationMinutes=60` is **a contract value, not evidence that independent Miami price feeds update hourly**. No such Florida live feed has been commissioned. The existing fare curve in `backend/market-pricing.js` is static, recorded evidence as of **2026-09-20**. Do not claim that the national platform currently automatically tracks competitors' quotes or refreshes pricing every hour.
- The common `backend/economics.js` models a minimum platform fee including Stripe, Connect, contingency and a small overhead allowance. It does **not** demonstrate actual full-company operating contribution at one million users; variable, legal and insurance costs must be reconciled with actual prices. No fare or payout formula is changed by this PR.

## Proposed cadence, by *publication* and actual need — not indiscriminate polling

1. **Government historical microdata:** On first market commissioning, record official publication cadence, revision lag and schema. Thereafter check inexpensive metadata (ETag / Last-Modified, dataset revision or release listing) near its scheduled release or daily only when the publisher's change behavior warrants; ingest incremental, bounded, verified new batches **only if source version changes**. A Chicago/NYC monthly data release does not need 24 automated raw imports per day.
2. **Licensed independent market-price feeds:** Enable **only after** contract/terms, geography, quote interpretation, rate limits and economics are verified. Use provider-published change events if offered, else bounded polling aligned to the provider's actual refresh SLA. Do not silently introduce a nationwide commercial API bill.
3. **Controlled field quote panels:** Use small, representative route/daypart/weekday panels at commissioning and on material drift or scheduled review. Never automate access to other companies' private services without permission. A contemporaneous market-specific field panel is validation, not automatically a production fare.
4. **Actual American Rider service signals:** Monitor route distance/time, available Operators, wait time, acceptance/completion, cancellation and local traffic around active requests, with privacy and reliable source freshness. These describe OUR marketplace conditions, not other companies' contemporaneous prices. Keep local operational quote economics separate from national competitor monitoring. No hidden surge or unbounded multiplier.
5. **Production promotion:** Compare source-independent medians across matched distance/time/service/daypart cells, reject data with weak provenance, delay/freshness or high source concentration, evaluate holdouts/short-trip equity, ensure exact routing/tolls/taxes/Connect fees and positive contributions, require recorded approval before changes. Avoid moving every city's fare because one country's public dataset changes.
6. **Economics and operations:** Cost unit = **(billed per-publication API+Firestore reads/writes+storage+manual sampling)/commissioned market**, and separately **per completed Travel / on-duty Operator-hour**; not a cost per research catalog city. Budget alerts should not shut off legal/compliance and money safety paths. Use a single phone exception dashboard and no AI calls on the normal quote path.

**National simulation acceptance:** An arbitrary research catalog of 1,000 cities with no approved collectors must fetch no public source and perform no Firestore operations; one real commissioned metro may import a bounded source revision without triggering unrelated metros; replayed/reordered identical samples generate no additional source-change checkpoint; changed fare values produce a new fingerprint and go through promotion gates; provider outage or stale sources never invent a fresh market price.
