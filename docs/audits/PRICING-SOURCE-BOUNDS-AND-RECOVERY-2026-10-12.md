## Actual defects identified in current released source
1. Both `backend/south-florida-public-rates.js` and `backend/south-florida-authorized-panel.js` claimed response-size limits, but implemented `await response.text()` BEFORE measuring actual bytes. Missing or false `Content-Length` left the fetch capable of allocating an unbounded decoded body. This is a resource-exhaustion failure mode even with redirect-blocked, approved sources. Proposed `bounded-source-response.js` reads a real byte stream and cancels immediately on the 300,000-/120,000-byte decoded ceilings respectively. Missing stream fails closed; normal short sources retain their exact meaning.
2. The public-source watcher persisted a seven-day `checkedAt` even after one or both sources went down; temporary failure could suppress retries for a week. Proposed repair only retries *unverified* results after **24 hours** (already the shared maintenance cadence); valid matching or conflicting source checks remain weekly. No busy loop, no unbounded provider polling, no HTTP during individual Travel quotation; retry persistence remains one small document.
3. The optional lawful field-panel connector still has **NO APPROVED LIVE SUPPLIER**. The existing source plan, multi-family condition-cell requirement, matched route/daypart evidence, immutable observation replay, holdout, economics and router gates must not be relaxed to manufacture a 90%-discount claim.
