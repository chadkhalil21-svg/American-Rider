# American Rider — Council and Institutional Standard

**Founder-ratified reference framework — 10 October 2026.**
**Scope:** Every technical architecture decision, Operator and Traveler workflow, Operations control, release gate, financial choice, screen, piece of copy, and future market expansion.

The **Council is a research and review rubric**, not a claim of personal consultation, formal endorsement, employment or advisory relationships with these people, universities, firms or the Emirates airline.

## Council

- **Strategic reference figures:** Henry Ford (repeatable process, standardization, affordable scale), John D. Rockefeller (sound operating economics, stewardship of capital, durable systems), and Steve Jobs (coherent end-to-end product, restraint, clarity and attention to the actual user experience). These are principles drawn from historical examples, not literal advice.
- **Institutional research/reference sources:** Harvard Business School, Stanford Graduate School of Business, MIT Sloan, leading universities and rigorous research organizations, Apple, leading software engineering and design organizations. Apply directly relevant, dated published guidance; do not imply any institution has approved an American Rider decision.
- **Hospitality benchmark:** Emirates and distinguished first-class service — composed, discreet, attentive, precise, personalized when relevant, and effortless for the customer. Benchmark the *care and consistency*, not an expensive imitation of luxury decoration or business costs.

## Nonnegotiable rubric

**Institutional. Authoritative. Sophisticated. Elegant. Seamless. Trustworthy. Economically disciplined. Slightly futuristic.**

The resulting product has **quiet authority**: useful sophistication without complexity on display. Warm but restrained hospitality, carefully chosen words, familiar controls, polished recovery from errors, respect for safety/privacy and no unearned claims.

The founder operates American Rider from **one iPhone**. National systems must perform routine compliance, screening, insurance, billing, dispatch and exception monitoring without founder intervention wherever dependable and lawful. Optimize for thousands to millions of participants, not a demo batch. Processing 200 records at a time is a bounded implementation detail, never certification of national throughput. Reuse deployed components and lower-cost available services; preserve live eligibility, market and legal gates.

## Required decision review — use on every meaningful change

1. **Ford / infrastructure:** What is the simplest repeatable, event-driven or otherwise genuinely scalable process? Can it avoid unnecessary whole-fleet rescans, bottlenecks, handoffs and re-entry?
2. **Rockefeller / operating economics:** What are actual per-Travel and per-Operator costs, worst-case scaling cost, and continuing obligations? Is a reliable lower-cost alternative already built?
3. **Jobs / Apple / human factors:** Is the shortest safe, understandable end-to-end user path implemented? Does every button do what its label says? Can the person recover without support?
4. **Harvard / Stanford / MIT / research:** Are service outcomes measurable, operationally financeable, based on real user needs and tested on the actual target population? Is this a verified fact or a design proposal?
5. **Emirates / hospitality:** Is the complete service dignified, composed and attentive, including loading, no-availability, delays, errors, disputes, cancellations and assistance?
6. **Institutional release standard:** Is every consequential state authoritative, independently verifiable and auditable? Is there exact-SHA and real-world evidence? Never claim law, insurance or CRA commissioning on the strength of code, synthetic tests or simulated screenshots.

Apply these standards across **every screen and state**, not a featured subset. Do not name marketplace rivals on public screens when ordinary neutral wording suffices. Avoid adding AI, subscriptions or human queues when deterministic existing infrastructure does the job.

## Published references (guidance, not endorsements)

- [Harvard Business School — Service Excellence by Design](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?research=7002): explicit service attributes, a mechanism that sustainably funds them, and a delivery system capable of meeting promises.
- [Stanford d.school — Design Thinking Bootleg](https://dschool.stanford.edu/tools/design-thinking-bootleg): empathize, define, ideate, prototype and test before declaring a service design ready.
- [Apple — Human Interface Guidelines, Design Principles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles/): purpose, agency, responsibility, familiarity, flexibility, simplicity, craft, delight; favor trustworthy clarity over decoration.
- [Emirates — First Class Experience](https://www.emirates.com/us/english/experience/cabin-features/first-class/b777/): personalized service, privacy, comfort, traveler choice and meticulous details, used here only as a benchmark of service quality.
- [Google SRE — Eliminating Toil](https://sre.google/sre-book/eliminating-toil/): engineer operational processes rather than staffing endless repetitive manual work.
