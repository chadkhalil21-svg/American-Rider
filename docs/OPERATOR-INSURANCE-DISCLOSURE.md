## Why you're getting this

Florida law (**Fla. Stat. § 627.748**) requires American Rider to tell you, **in writing and
before your first paid ride**, two things:
1. What insurance American Rider carries while you drive, and
2. That **your own personal car insurance may NOT cover you** while you are logged into the
   American Rider app or giving a ride.

Please read it, ask questions, and sign at the bottom before you start driving.

---

## The one thing you must understand

**Your normal personal auto policy probably will NOT cover you while you're working.**

Florida law specifically **allows** personal auto insurers to refuse coverage the moment you
turn on a rideshare app or pick up a paying rider. So if you drive for money on a personal-only
policy and something happens, your insurer can **deny the claim** — leaving you personally on the
hook for the damage.

That is why the coverage below matters, and why you need the right insurance **before** you drive.

## What coverage applies, and when

Florida splits your time into three periods based on what the app is doing:

| Period | When | Minimum coverage Florida requires | Who provides it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **App OFF** | You're driving for yourself, app closed | Your normal personal auto policy | **You** |
| **Period 1** | App ON, waiting — no ride accepted yet | **$50,000** per person / **$100,000** per incident (injury) + **$25,000** property damage, plus **PIP** and **uninsured/underinsured motorist** | **You** — usually via a *rideshare endorsement* on your personal policy |
| **Periods 2 & 3** | From the moment you accept a ride, through dropping the rider off | **$1,000,000** primary liability | **American Rider** — `[[ insurer name ]]`, policy `[[ policy # ]]` |

*(Note: the $1,000,000 applies from the second you accept the ride — not just when the rider is
in the car.)*

## What YOU are required to carry

You must have **one** of the following, and American Rider verifies it during sign-up:

- **A rideshare endorsement** added to your personal auto policy *(the low-cost option — a small
  add-on that keeps your policy valid during Period 1)*, **or**
- **A commercial / for-hire ("livery") auto policy** *(fuller coverage, higher cost — usually for
  full-time drivers)*.

You must keep this coverage active the whole time you drive with American Rider, and carry proof
of it in your vehicle at all times (Florida requires this).

**What to do:** Call your auto insurer and ask, *"Do you offer a rideshare endorsement, and does
it cover me driving for a Transportation Network Company in Florida?"* If they don't, ask a
licensed Florida agent about a rideshare endorsement or a commercial for-hire policy.
