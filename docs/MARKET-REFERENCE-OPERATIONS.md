# National market-reference operations

Status: launch pricing operating contract.

American Rider maintains two deliberately separate clocks.

## External market reference

Observe eligible external evidence hourly when a lawful/public/licensed source supports that cadence. Recompute the robust condition-matched reference hourly. Promote a production reference no more than daily, and only after the existing evidence, source-concentration, router, hold-out and economics gates pass.

Eligible sources follow the existing hierarchy: usable government/public passenger-fare microdata first; controlled contemporaneous observations of prices publicly offered to ordinary consumers where government fare microdata is unavailable; independent public rate-card/observed-price publications as corroboration; licensed data only as an optional later gap-fill. Never scrape or automate access contrary to source terms, and never let one competitor, one route, one quote or one publication determine production. Never ingest competitors' nonpublic competitively sensitive data or participate in a shared competitor pricing/recommendation system; American Rider makes its pricing decisions independently.\n\nA controlled public-price panel must span multiple providers, representative routes and time cells. Promotions, tips, tolls and government pass-throughs are normalized out before comparison. Observations retain provider/source provenance and timestamp. Robust medians and outlier rejection are required; a source-concentration gate prevents any one provider from controlling the reference. Gridwise is deferred and is not required for the public-first launch path.

If no fresh eligible external evidence exists, retain the last verified production reference and mark freshness/coverage degraded. Do not invent a market movement.

## American Rider real-time marketplace

At quote time, evaluate authoritative routed duration/traffic plus American Rider's own request pressure, available Operators, pickup ETA, acceptance/completion, pickup burden and destination conditions. Traffic and own-marketplace snapshots older than five minutes are stale. Weather older than 15 minutes and event-calendar data older than 60 minutes are stale.

These signals may support a future bounded market-balance adjustment only after sufficient American Rider observations exist. They do not change the 90% market-reference objective and they do not authorize an unbounded surge multiplier.

## Stability and governance

- national positioning objective: 90% of the comparable robust local reference;
- no personalized pricing and no protected-class signals or proxies;
- no default market and no cross-market coefficient inheritance;
- at least two independent evidence families for automatic promotion;
- router, hold-out and economics gates must pass;
- a proposed reference movement greater than 10% requires manual review;
- production reference promotion occurs at most daily;
- all observations, recomputations, promotions, overrides and source provenance must be auditable.

## Declared emergencies

A declared emergency disables upward dynamic market-balance adjustment in the affected geography until the applicable legal/regulatory review says otherwise. Preserve the prior 30-day baseline needed for emergency-price review and retain evidence of cost/market changes. Jurisdiction-specific law may impose stricter controls and always overrides this floor.

## Provider boundary

This contract does not pretend American Rider has a universal competitor-price API. Production deployment must connect approved evidence collectors/provider feeds for each commissioned market. Missing collector credentials or unavailable evidence is a commissioning/evidence condition, not permission to fabricate or inherit another market's reference.
